# Benchmark results

200 scenarios, 8 edits each. Generated by `benchmark/generate.py`; scores in `benchmark/data/`.

## Pairwise accuracy

How often the metric scores a meaning-preserving edit above a meaning-breaking edit of the same scenario. 0.5 is a coin toss; 1.0 is perfect.

| Metric | Pairwise accuracy | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---|
| G-BLEU | 0.687 | 0.681 to 0.692 |
| word BLEU | 0.594 | 0.591 to 0.596 |
| sacreBLEU | 0.601 | 0.596 to 0.606 |
| ROUGE-L | 0.703 | 0.696 to 0.710 |
